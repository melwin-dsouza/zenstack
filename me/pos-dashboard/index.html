POS Dashboard

Making point-of-sale data easier to understand and manage.

What it does

A POS management platform with a web dashboard and mobile applications for sales tracking, inventory and reporting. The goal is simple: take operational data from a retail system and make it accessible through a cleaner interface for people who actually run the business.

  • Real-time sales tracking across a web dashboard
  • Inventory management and stock visibility
  • Reporting for day-to-day business decisions
  • Matching iOS and Android apps on the same backend
