How I approach engineering

Practical over impressive

Start with the problem

I first try to understand what is actually inefficient, expensive, repetitive or difficult today.

Keep the solution practical

The best architecture isn't necessarily the most complicated one. I prefer solutions that are understandable, maintainable and appropriate for the problem.

Build for production

Security, failure handling, observability, deployment and maintenance are part of building the system — not things to think about at the end.

Own the outcome

I like being responsible for the complete system. If something doesn't work in production, I want to understand why and fix the actual problem.

Good engineering isn't about using more technology. It's about using the right technology to solve the problem.
