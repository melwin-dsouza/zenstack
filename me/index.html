// what I actually do

From business problem to working system

Turn business problems into software

A business process that currently lives in Excel, email or manual work can often become a proper application. I design and build the system around the actual workflow rather than starting with a technology.

Build the backend that makes applications work

I specialise in APIs, business logic, integrations, authentication, data processing and distributed systems — the parts users may not see, but that make an application reliable.

Connect systems that don't naturally talk to each other

POS systems, databases, third-party APIs, identity providers and cloud services often need to work together. I build the integrations and workflows that connect them — and design user-centric dashboards or an entire ERP/CRM if needed. I can also build Android and iOS apps.

Take software all the way to production

I can provision the infrastructure, deploy the application, configure domains and SSL, monitor it and maintain it after launch.

In simple terms: give me a business problem, and I can help turn it into a working system.
